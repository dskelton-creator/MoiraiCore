Welcome to MoiraiCore

      
Your local AI orchestration platform. MoiraiCore connects multiple AI agents, automates goal execution, manages memory vaults, and coordinates projects — all from a single dashboard.

Goals & Agents

      
Create goals and MoiraiCore decomposes them into tasks for specialized agents (Hermes, Researcher, Writer, Developer). Track progress in real-time with automatic verification.
